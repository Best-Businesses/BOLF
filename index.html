Why We Exist

Our Mission

Bread of Life Farms exists to help individuals and families experiencing homelessness achieve stability and self-sufficiency. We acquire and develop land for certified agricultural production, grow and sell farm products, and use the proceeds to fund compassionate programs including sack lunches, food boxes, food pantries, and structured encampments with restrooms, showers, and supportive services.

Through practical help and life skills development, we walk alongside participants — and upon successful completion of our program, we assist them in obtaining a small piece of land and building a tiny home so they can transition into long-term independence.

Mentors & “Open Ear” Companions

We need mentors for homeless individuals — people willing to simply be an open ear. Many future residents have experienced isolation and trauma. We need Christian mentors willing to walk alongside them: to listen, pray, share life skills, and offer consistent emotional support.

  • One-on-one discipleship & encouragement
  • Assist with goal setting (education, finances, health)
  • Just being an open ear, without judgment

Farm & Facility Builders

Before residents arrive, we need to construct the physical heart of the community — campsites, trails, kitchens, and more.

  • Building primitive campsites for retreats & transitional phases
  • Creating accessible nature trails across farmland
  • Kitchen duties for volunteer crews and future community dinners
  • Erosion control, fencing, greenhouse assembly
  • Compost station construction & gardening infrastructure

No experience necessary — just a willing heart and hands.
